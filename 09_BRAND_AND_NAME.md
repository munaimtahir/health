# Brand and Naming

## Recommended name
**Vexel Health Passport**

## Recommended Play title
**Vexel Health Passport: Records**

## Tagline
**Your health history, organized.**

## Package name
`pk.vexel.healthpassport`

## Brand position
A calm, private, reliable personal health-history organizer—not a diagnostic AI application.

## Visual direction
- White and very light neutral backgrounds
- Calm blue/green primary accents
- Clear typography
- Rounded cards
- Strong spacing
- Minimal red, reserved for urgent user-entered flags or destructive actions

## Icon direction
A shield or folded record containing a subtle timeline line. Avoid a generic medical cross as the only idea, and avoid robot/AI imagery.

## Voice
- Clear
- Neutral
- Reassuring without medical promises
- Plain language
- No alarmist interpretation

## Naming alternatives retained
- Vexel Health Vault
- Vexel Care Timeline
- Sehat Passport by Vexel
- My Vexel Health

Final recommendation: retain **Vexel Health Passport** unless formal trademark or store checks identify a conflict.
