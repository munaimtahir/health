# Deferred Features

Status: Canonical. Purpose: protect scaffold scope.

Deferred: diagnostic AI, treatment or medication recommendations, telemedicine, clinician portal, multiple profiles, community, advertising, hospital or insurance integrations, advanced wearables, cloud-first architecture, mandatory accounts, unrestricted health chatbot, OCR, and Health Connect. Future AI/OCR must be optional, consented, preserve originals, and require confirmation of suggestions.
