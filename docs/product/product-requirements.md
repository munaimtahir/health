# Product Requirements

Status: Canonical. Purpose: define the product behavior that the scaffold prepares for.

The application supports one individual with a personal health profile, allergies, diagnoses, procedures, symptoms, medical documents, medication history, consultations, reminders, a unified timeline, appointment preparation, PDF summaries, export, local backup/restore, deletion, and application lock. Core functionality must work without internet access and without a mandatory account.

User-facing text must use **Vexel Health Passport** and the tagline **Your health history, organized.** Imported originals remain unchanged; structured data is user-controlled and traceable to its source.
