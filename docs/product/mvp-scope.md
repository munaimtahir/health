# MVP Scope

Status: Canonical. Purpose: distinguish required MVP capability from scaffold placeholders.

MVP includes a single-user profile; medical information, allergies, diagnoses, procedures; timeline; structured symptom logging with severity, timing, duration, ongoing state, body location, associated symptoms, observed triggers, related medication, episode identifiers, and optional private image attachments; medical-record vault with private import, metadata, replacement, preview/open, and scoped sharing; structured medication history with dose-change, stop, and restart periods; follow-up planner and notifications; appointment preparation and selectable/date-ranged PDF summary; password-encrypted local backup/restore with integrity validation; date-ranged structured and readable export; app lock; deletion; accessibility; release hardening; and offline-first operation. These are required MVP capabilities, not future backlog items. Play Console upload and publication remain manual owner actions after engineering and internal-testing acceptance. Feature behavior is scheduled by [`../delivery/development-sprints.md`](../delivery/development-sprints.md).
