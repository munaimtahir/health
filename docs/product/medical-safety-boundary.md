# Medical Safety Boundary

Status: Canonical. Purpose: constrain health-related wording and logic.

The app records and organizes information supplied by the user. It must use neutral language such as “occurred around the same time” and identify user-entered plans. It must not infer disease, causation, urgency, dosage changes, or follow-up intervals. The app disclaimer: “This application helps users record and organize symptoms, treatments and personal health information. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations or emergency assessment.”
