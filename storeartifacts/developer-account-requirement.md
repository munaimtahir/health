# Google Play developer account requirement

## Determination

Vexel Health Passport should be published from a verified **organization developer account**, not a personal developer account.

Google Play's account-type guidance identifies health apps, including medical apps, as apps whose developers should choose an organization account. Google Play's announced requirements make organization registration mandatory for developers providing health apps from **2026-09-30**.

Vexel falls within that scope because its core purpose includes:

- maintaining personal health records and documents;
- recording symptoms and health timelines;
- managing medications and medication reminders;
- tracking appointments; and
- creating health summaries and reports.

These functions map directly to the Health apps declaration categories **Healthcare Services and Management** and **Medication and Treatment Management**. The app's non-diagnostic scope and non-medical-device disclaimer remain necessary, but they do not remove the app from Google's health-app classification.

## Organization account prerequisites

Prepare organization details that match the authoritative business records:

- legal organization name and address;
- organization phone number and website;
- developer contact name, email address, and phone number;
- public developer email address and phone number; and
- a D-U-N-S number for the organization.

Google advises that obtaining a D-U-N-S number may take up to 30 days. Start this process before the intended submission date.

## Publishing path

- If no Play Console app has been created for `com.vexel.passport`, establish and verify the organization account before creating it.
- If the app already exists in a personal account, use Google's supported app-transfer process to move it to the verified organization account, subject to transfer eligibility, or contact Play Console support.
- Do not attempt to avoid the requirement by changing the store category, app name, or disclaimer while retaining health-management functionality.
