# Feature graphic generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing

Asset type: Google Play feature graphic for an Android personal health-record organizer, final canvas 1024 by 500 pixels, 24-bit PNG without alpha.

Primary request: Create a polished, calm, trustworthy feature graphic for Vexel Health Passport, an offline-first private personal health-record organizer. Communicate organization, privacy and user control without implying diagnosis, treatment or medical-device functionality.

Scene/backdrop: A rich but calm teal-to-deep-navy gradient derived from `#0F766E`, with soft mint and pale aqua atmospheric shapes. Avoid pure white, black and dark gray backgrounds.

Subject: A clean central arrangement of four abstract, rounded health-record cards representing a symptom timeline, medication schedule, private document and reminder calendar. Include subtle document, checkmark, lock and calendar motifs as original generic symbols. The cards should feel like an extension of the app's Material 3 visual language, not screenshots and not a phone mockup.

Style/medium: Premium editorial 3D-paper illustration with soft depth, crisp rounded geometry, gentle shadows and restrained detail. Friendly and contemporary, suitable for a privacy-focused medical organizer.

Composition/framing: Very wide 1024:500 composition. Keep all meaningful cards and symbols within the central 70 percent. Use edge elements only as low-detail background decoration. Maintain clean negative space and immediate readability at small size.

Lighting/mood: Soft studio illumination, calm, private, organized and reassuring.

Color palette: Teal `#0F766E`, dark navy `#0F172A`, mint `#CCFBF1`, pale slate `#F8FAFC`, white card surfaces and small warm amber accents.

Text: Prefer no text in the generated artwork. The visual must communicate the app experience without copy and remain reusable across locales.

Constraints: 1024 by 500 final crop; no alpha; center-safe composition; no real patient information; no red emergency cross; no realistic pills with imprints; no hospital or clinician endorsement; no app-store badges; no ratings, awards, price or promotional language.

Avoid: phone or device frames, screenshots, tiny labels, illegible pseudo-text, medical diagnosis imagery, treatment claims, AI imagery, cloud symbols, charts implying clinical interpretation, watermarks, third-party logos and clutter.
