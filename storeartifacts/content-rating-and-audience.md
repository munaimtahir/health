# Content rating and target audience worksheet

These are proposed answers based on the current implementation. The Play Console owner must review the exact questionnaire presented at submission time.

## Target audience

- Recommended target age: **18 and over**
- Primary audience: adults organizing their own personal health information.
- The app is not designed specifically for children and should not be enrolled in the Families program without a separate legal, safety and UX review.

## Content questionnaire expectations

- Violence or graphic content: **No**
- Sexual content or nudity: **No**
- Profanity or crude humor: **No**
- Gambling: **No**
- Controlled substances promotion or sales: **No**
- User-to-user communication: **No**
- Public user-generated content: **No**
- Location sharing: **No**
- In-app purchases: **No**
- Advertising: **No**
- Unrestricted internet access or web browsing: **No**

The app can contain private health information entered by the user, including medication and diagnosis names. That content is not supplied by the developer, published publicly, or shared with other users by default.
