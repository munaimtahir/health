## Full description

Keep your personal health history organized in one private, offline-first place.

Vexel Health Passport helps you record and review information you enter yourself, including symptoms, medications, documents, reminders, appointments and profile details. Core features work without an account or internet connection, and your records remain in app-private storage on your device unless you explicitly export or share them.

ORGANIZE YOUR HEALTH HISTORY

- Record symptoms with timing, severity, duration, notes and optional attachments.
- Keep structured medication details and a history of changes, stops and restarts.
- Import PDF and image records into a private document vault.
- Review user-entered events in a searchable health timeline.

PLAN AND PREPARE

- Create your own medication, appointment, follow-up and custom reminders.
- Generate date-ranged PDF summaries for appointments.
- Select which sections to include before creating a report.

CONTROL YOUR DATA

- Export structured data or a readable summary when you choose.
- Create password-encrypted local backups and restore them later.
- Delete individual records or erase all app-managed data from the device.
- Use an optional PIN or supported device authentication to lock the app.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

- No mandatory account.
- No advertising SDK.
- No analytics or health-event telemetry.
- No cloud sync or developer server in the current release.
- No network connection required for core features.

Vexel Health Passport is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations or emergency assessment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and contact local emergency services in an emergency.
