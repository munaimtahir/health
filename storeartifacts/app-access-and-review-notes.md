# App access and Google Play review notes

## Access

- No account, login, subscription or network connection is required.
- No reviewer credentials are required.
- The app works offline.
- The optional PIN and device-authentication lock are configured by the user and are not enabled before onboarding.

## Suggested review flow

1. Launch the app.
2. Read the non-diagnostic onboarding disclosure.
3. Select the acknowledgement checkbox and continue.
4. Use synthetic information only to exercise profile, symptom, medication, document, reminder, report, export and backup features.
5. Notification permission may be denied; local record features remain available.
6. To test a reminder notification, grant notification permission and create a reminder due shortly.
7. To test app lock, set a PIN in Settings. Device authentication appears only when supported and enrolled on the test device.
8. Use Settings > Delete all app data to remove all synthetic test content.

## Safety context for reviewers

The app records and organizes information entered by the user. It does not diagnose conditions, interpret results, recommend medication changes, calculate doses, choose follow-up intervals, provide emergency assessment or replace professional care.
