# Proposed Google Play Health apps declaration

Complete the Health apps declaration for closed, open, or production distribution.

## Recommended health features

Select:

- **Healthcare Services and Management** — the app manages user-entered health records, appointments, reminders, reports and healthcare logistics.
- **Medication and Treatment Management** — the app records medication schedules and history and supports user-created medication reminders.

Do not select unless future functionality changes:

- Clinical Decision Support
- Medical Reference and Education
- Medical Device Apps
- Emergency and First Aid
- Health subjects research
- Activity and Fitness, Nutrition, Sleep, Period Tracking, or Stress Management

## Regulatory position

Proposed answer: **The app is not a medical device.** The owner must confirm this for every distribution jurisdiction before submission.

The app does not diagnose, interpret clinical results, recommend treatment, calculate doses, determine urgency, recommend follow-up intervals, connect to medical hardware, or use device sensors as a medical instrument.

## Required explanation and disclaimer

Vexel Health Passport is an offline-first personal health-record organizer for information entered by the user. It supports record organization, medication history, user-created reminders, private document storage, reports, exports and encrypted local backups.

Vexel Health Passport is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations or emergency assessment. Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

## Health data access

- No Health Connect access.
- No body-sensor permission.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone or SMS permission.
- Health information is entered or imported by the user and stored locally.
- No developer-side health-data collection or sharing in the current release.
